Fernanda Biagi

Born in Mexico,
formed in Milan

I grew up surrounded by color, myth and a little bit of magic. Then Milan taught me restraint: clean lines, quiet space, the discipline of fashion. My paintings are what happens when those two worlds meet and neither one backs down.

I paint in oil, by hand, one piece at a time, and I stop when there's nothing left to take away. Each canvas is a way of looking inward. That's what mirada means to me.
